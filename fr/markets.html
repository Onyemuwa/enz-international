TZ · Tanzania

China sourcing, factory setup, and import support for businesses in Tanzania.

From Dar es Salaam to Dodoma, we help Tanzanian businesses source directly from vetted Chinese manufacturers, set up local production, and navigate import logistics through the port of Dar es Salaam.

KE · Kenya

China sourcing, factory setup, and import support for businesses in Kenya.

We support Kenyan importers and manufacturers with supplier vetting, quality control, and factory-setup consulting — coordinated through our Nairobi presence and Guangzhou sourcing hub.

CD · Democratic Republic of Congo

China sourcing, commodity trading, and factory setup support in the DRC.

Working out of Kinshasa, we connect DRC-based businesses and commodity traders with Chinese manufacturing and procurement channels, alongside factory-setup consulting for local production.

US · United States

A China sourcing partner for US-based importers and brands.

We help US businesses find and vet Chinese manufacturers, manage quality control and freight forwarding, and evaluate factory-setup options for diversifying production.

UK · United Kingdom

A China sourcing partner for UK-based importers and brands.

Our London presence supports UK businesses with Chinese supplier sourcing, quality control, and consulting on factory setup and supply-chain diversification.
