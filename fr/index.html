01

Le problème

L'approvisionnement en Chine échoue de façon prévisible

Quatre raisons, et aucune n'est de la malchance.

You cannot verify who you are buying from

Trading companies present themselves as factories. Platform badges are not verification.

Quality is checked once, too late to fix

One inspection at the end tells you it is wrong once it is made and paid for.

The quoted price is not the price you pay

Freight, duties and port fees arrive later. A cheap EXW quote often lands dearer than a higher FOB one.

Nobody owns the problem when it goes wrong

Supplier, forwarder and broker each answer for their own leg. The gaps between them are yours.

Processus

Comment se déroule un projet d'approvisionnement

Cinq étapes du premier appel à la livraison, avec ce qui se passe à chacune et ce que vous en retirez.

  1. 01 30 minutes

    Discovery call

    What you are buying, at what volume, and by when.

  2. 02 1–2 weeks

    Supplier identification

    Factories shortlisted against your spec, and verified.

  3. 03 2–4 weeks

    Sampling and negotiation

    Samples are produced and reviewed before terms are fixed.

  4. 04 Varies by product

    Production and quality control

    Inspected at defined checkpoints, not just at the end.

  5. 05 2–6 weeks in transit

    Freight and customs

    Goods are booked, documented, and cleared into your market.
