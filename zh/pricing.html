成本

影响报价的因素

每个项目都在沟通电话后报价，因为这四个因素差异过大，公布价目表无法做到准确。

1

Scope

Sourcing only, sourcing plus logistics, or full factory setup. The more legs we own, the more of the work moves off your desk.

2

Order value and volume

A single container and a rolling monthly programme need different amounts of oversight, and are priced differently.

3

Product complexity

Commodity goods to a standard specification take less verification than custom-engineered or regulated products.

4

Inspection depth

How many of the four inspection points you want covered, and whether the order justifies on-site factory visits.

本页为何没有价格

为采购与贸易业务公布价目表，对大多数读者而言都是不准确的。我们在 30 分钟通话后以书面形式报价，签署任何文件之前完成——如果项目并不合适，我们会直说，而不是先报价。

如何开始

联系我们之后会发生什么

在书面方案确认之前，你无需做出任何承诺。以下是从第一条消息到项目启动的完整流程。

  1. 01

    Tell us what you need

    2 minutes

    Product, rough volume, target market, and your timeline. A paragraph is enough — you do not need a finished specification to start.

  2. 02

    We reply within one business day

    1 business day

    A person reads it and comes back with either questions or an honest "this is not a fit for us". No drip sequence, no sales cadence.

  3. 03

    A 30-minute scoping call

    30 minutes

    We work out what the project actually involves and whether we are the right people for it. Free, and with no obligation attached.

  4. 04

    A written scope, then work begins

    Before you commit

    What we will do, what it costs, and how long it takes — in writing. Nothing starts until you have agreed to it.
