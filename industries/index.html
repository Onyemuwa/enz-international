Industries

Industries we source for

Each category fails in its own way. These are the ones we work in most and what tends to go wrong in each.

Book Consultation Contact

Metal, hardware & electrical

Fasteners, wire, cable and small-metalware production lines.

What to watch

  • Material grade and coating standards for the destination market
  • Electrical safety marking and plug standards
  • Tooling wear and spare-parts supply

Food & snack production

Bakery, snack, noodle and packaged-food lines.

What to watch

  • Food-contact material certification
  • Hygienic design and easy cleaning
  • Local raw-material supply and ingredient specification

Beverage & bottled water

Water, soft drink, juice and alcohol bottling lines.

What to watch

  • Water treatment suited to your source water
  • Bottle, cap and label supply chain
  • Regulatory approval for drinking water and beverages

Plastics & rubber products

Moulding, blowing and extrusion lines for plastic goods and pipe.

What to watch

  • Resin supply and grade for the product
  • Mould and tooling lead time
  • Power requirement and cooling water

Paper, tissue & stationery

Tissue, paper-product and stationery lines.

What to watch

  • Pulp or waste-paper supply
  • Water and effluent handling
  • Output matched to local demand

Hygiene & personal care

Soap, detergent, hygiene and medical-disposable lines.

What to watch

  • Product registration with the local regulator
  • Raw-material and packaging supply
  • Cleanroom or hygiene-zone requirements

Recycling & waste-to-energy

Plastic and tyre recycling and fuel-briquette equipment.

What to watch

  • Environmental permits and emissions limits
  • Reliable feedstock supply
  • Safe handling and fire protection

Construction & building materials

Fixtures, tiles, sanitaryware, hardware and finishing materials.

What to watch

  • Freight cost often exceeds unit cost — container optimisation matters more than unit price
  • Dimensional consistency across production runs
  • Destination building-standard compliance

Industrial equipment & machinery

Production machinery, spare parts and assembly-line equipment.

What to watch

  • Voltage, frequency, and plug standards for the destination market
  • Spare-parts availability and after-sales support terms
  • Installation, commissioning, and operator training

Consumer goods & retail

Household products and general merchandise.

What to watch

  • Tighter AQL limits — defects reach end customers directly
  • Packaging and labelling compliance for the destination market
  • Seasonal lead-time planning around Chinese New Year

Commodities & raw materials

Copper, cobalt and select agricultural goods.

What to watch

  • Quality specification and assay method agreed in writing
  • Chain-of-custody documentation from point of origin
  • Clear force majeure and off-spec penalty clauses

Agriculture & food processing

Post-harvest processing, milling and packaged-food production equipment.

What to watch

  • Food-contact material certification for the destination market
  • Hygienic design and cleaning-in-place requirements
  • Seasonal raw-material supply and storage capacity

Packaging & printing

Flexible packaging, labelling and print-finishing equipment for branded goods.

What to watch

  • Ink and substrate compliance for food or pharma packaging
  • Colour-matching consistency across production runs
  • Minimum order quantities on custom print tooling

Textiles & apparel

Fabric production and garment manufacturing equipment.

What to watch

  • Fabric shrinkage and colourfastness testing before bulk production
  • Compliance labelling for the destination market
  • Cutting capacity versus order lead time
