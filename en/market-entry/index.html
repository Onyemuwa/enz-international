Frequently Asked Questions

How does the sourcing process start?

It starts with a consultation where you share your product specifications, target price range, and order volume. From there we shortlist and vet suppliers on your behalf.

Do you handle quality control and inspections?

Yes. We offer staged quality control — incoming material checks, in-process inspection, and pre-shipment inspection — so defects are caught before goods leave the factory.

Can you help set up a factory outside of China?

Yes, our factory-setup service covers site selection, machinery sourcing, assembly-line planning, and commissioning support, including in East African markets.

What information do you need for a factory-setup consultation?

A general sense of your target product, expected production volume, and preferred location is enough to start. We refine the scope together during the consultation.
