What moves a quote

Quoted after a scoping call, because these four vary too much to publish.

1

Scope

Sourcing, sourcing plus logistics, or full factory setup.

2

Order value and volume

One container and a monthly programme need different oversight.

3

Product complexity

Standard goods need less verification than custom or regulated ones.

4

Inspection depth

How many of the four inspection points you want covered.

Why there are no prices on this page

A published rate card for sourcing and trading work would be wrong for most of the people reading it. We quote after a 30-minute call, in writing, before anything is signed — and if the project is not a fit, we say so instead of quoting.
